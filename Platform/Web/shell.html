Space Cadet Pinball

A port of 3D Pinball for Windows, running in your browser. It needs the original game data, which isn't included.

1. Add the game data. Choose PINBALL.DAT with its .WAV and .MID files from 3D Pinball for Windows, or the folder of Full Tilt! Pinball (CADET.DAT).

The files stay in this browser (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded anywhere.

2. Music. The music is MIDI and needs a SoundFont.

(MIT licence, by Frank Wen and Michael Cowgill)

Or add your own .sf2 or .sf3 with the game files above. Music needs a lot of memory.

Source: github.com/simonsteiner/SpaceCadetPinball. 3D Pinball for Windows is Microsoft's; this page contains none of its files.
