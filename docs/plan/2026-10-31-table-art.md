# Table art plan

Date: 2026-10-31. Status: decided; in review: T1 as #159, T2 as #161, T3 as #162.

The test builds (see the [test builds plan](2026-10-30-test-builds.md)) turned up three wishes for Space Cadet 2026. 3D Pinball's Space Cadet has no picture in the table lists. Space Cadet 2026 can only change tables from the title. And its score panel, the HUD, has lost each table's own art, which Classic's panel shows. This plan covers all three. It is part of the [Space Cadet 2026 plan](2026-10-20-space-cadet-2026.md) and keeps its rules: the same physics and rules, the replays matching in both editions, and Classic one Tab away.

The art follows that plan's rule for art: it comes from the player's own DAT file, cut out when the game starts, and is never saved, committed or uploaded. Screenshots that show it stay out of pull requests; describe what was checked instead.

## Decided

- **3D Pinball's Space Cadet gets its score panel's logo as its picture** (2026-10-31). The playfield made a busy picture at 120 by 120; the "3D Pinball Space Cadet" logo is what the panel shows. The picture is cut off above the ball's label and its digit box, which is empty outside play. Any other table without a picture gets its playfield.
- **Space Cadet 2026 can change tables during a game** (2026-10-31): a Change Table row on the pause screen, and Ctrl+T, which until now only Classic had.
- **The HUD gets a header with the table's own panel art** (2026-10-31), option A of the four brainstormed: an art header above the HUD's content. Not chosen: the panel art dimmed behind the whole HUD (B), a header and a footer (C), and the original panel itself next to Space Cadet 2026's table (D), which could come later as a setting.
- **The header keeps a readable size, and has a switch** (2026-10-31, in #162's review). The HUD's contents give up to a tenth of their size to keep the header at least 110 pixels high; Settings → Graphics → Panel art turns it off.
- **Dragon's Keep's header is a composite** (2026-10-31). Its panel's art has no name in it, unlike the other three, so the "Dragon's Keep" wordmark from the bottom of its panel is keyed onto the bottom of the dragon art, over a soft dark shadow. The keyed version was chosen over the wordmark stacked under the art.

## T1: a picture for 3D Pinball's Space Cadet

Done in #159. `MakeTablePicture` in `winmain.cpp` makes a picture for a table that has no BMP next to its DAT file.
- **3D Pinball:** the logo from `PINBALL.DAT`'s `background` bitmap: x 394, y 20, 185 by 126 in the 600 by 416 frame.
- **Any other table without a BMP:** the `table` group's playfield.
- Both are coloured with the table's palette (entries 10 to 245, as `gdrv::display_palette` sets them), averaged down to fit 120 by 120 and centred on black.

`docs/setup.md` says where each table's picture comes from.

## T2: Change Table on the pause screen

- **The row:** a Change Table row on Space Cadet 2026's pause screen, in the lower group with Restart and Quit to title, because it ends the game in progress.
  - It opens the title's Table screen, with each table's picture, source and best score.
  - Choosing another table asks "End this game?", as Restart does, then loads it as the title's Table screen does.
  - Choosing the table already loaded, or Back, returns to the pause screen.
- **Ctrl+T** opens the same screen in Space Cadet 2026: from a game it pauses first, as Classic's Select Table does. The web build has no Ctrl+T, since the browser takes it.
- **Only with more than one table:** with the data of a single table, neither the row nor Ctrl+T does anything, as on the title.
- **Not while a recording plays back** in the Recordings viewer, where loading another table would end the playback.
- **Words:** the new texts go through `translations.cpp` in English, as the Phase B texts did, translated with F7.

Done when the row and Ctrl+T reach every table from a game in both layouts (wide and portrait), the confirmation keeps the game when declined, and the replays match.

## T3: the panel art header

1. **The art, cut out by the game.** At startup, for the table loaded, `winmain` cuts the header art from the DAT's `background` bitmap, at the first resolution, coloured with the table's palette as T1 does. It hands the art to the HUD as a texture through `DefinitiveHost`, as the table pictures go to the Table screen. The HUD reads only the view and the host, as before.
2. **One area per table,** in the first resolution's 600 by 416 frame. The mockup measured them on screen; #162 measured them again on the bitmaps. The `background` bitmap holds only the panel, 203 or 208 pixels wide at 386,12, so the on-screen areas of the Full Tilt! tables started left of it; the areas keep inside the panel's rim, so its frame lines stay out:

   | Table | Header art | Area (x, y, width, height) | Rim left out |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 3D Pinball's Space Cadet | The logo, as T1 | 394, 20, 185, 126 | the grey frame |
   | Full Tilt!'s Space Cadet (`CADET.DAT`) | The "SPACE CADET" arc and the ship | 388, 14, 199, 152 | the textured edge, 2 pixels |
   | Dragon's Keep | The dragon's head and neck, the knight at the bottom right, with the wordmark keyed on (below) | art 388, 30, 205, 158; wordmark 392, 351, 193, 59 | a gold line and a dark one inside it |
   | Skulduggery | The title and the two pirates, above the skull and the treasure | 387, 13, 206, 131 | the gold line, and the digit box's below |

   Full Tilt!'s demo (`DEMO.DAT`) gets no header: there is no copy here to check its panel against `CADET.DAT`'s.
3. **Dragon's Keep's composite.**
   - **The key:** each palette entry is marked once as the wordmark's (its gold letters and their dark red shadow) or as the background's (the yellow-green texture, the blue windows, near black). So the key is the same on every machine and doesn't depend on screen pixels.
   - **The rule the mockup used,** to be checked on the palette: gold is red above 110, blue below 55% of red, green below 85% of red; the shadow is red above 60, green and blue below 55% of red.
   - **The mask** is cleaned of single pixels and its edge softened by a pixel.
   - **Placing it:** the wordmark goes centred at the bottom of the art, over its own mask widened and blurred into a black shadow at 75%.
4. **In the HUD.**
   - **Placement:** the header sits at the top of the card, as wide as the content, at most about a quarter of the card's height, with a thin rim in the HUD's line colour. The content moves down by the header's height.
   - **Tight space:** the card grows into the window's margin for the header, and the contents shrink by up to a tenth to keep it at least 110 pixels high at scale 1. With less room still, the header shrinks and is left out below a minimum size.
   - **Scaling:** with Upscaled Art on, the header is enlarged with the art's upscaler (`Upscale`, MMPX), once for each Upscaled Art Size, the first time it is drawn at that size, so changing the setting during a game takes effect; without it, it scales smoothly.
   - **Left out** in the portrait layout's band and with the high-contrast HUD, whose text stays on plain black.
   - **On or off:** the header is on by default; Settings → Graphics → Panel art turns it off.
5. **Docs:** `docs/setup.md` describes the header and where its art comes from.

Done when each of the four tables shows its header in the wide layout, at 1×, 2× and 4×, with Upscaled Art on and off; the portrait band and the high-contrast HUD show none; Tab still shows Classic's exact frame; and the replays match in both editions.
